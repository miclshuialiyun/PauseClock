Frequently asked

I paid for Pro but it is still locked

Open Settings → Membership → Restore purchases.

I have a new phone. Do I have to pay again?

No. Restore purchases on the new device.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Export on the old device, import on the new one.

The timer looks wrong, or a record is flagged

PauseClock flags it rather than silently changing anything.

Do I need an internet connection?

No — everything works offline except purchases.

The widget is not updating

Open the app once to push a fresh snapshot.

Reminders are not arriving

Check the toggle, the permission, and quiet hours.

I want to use weight but not Health

Health integration is off by default.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data → Erase all data.

To help us fix things quickly, please include:

  • Device model and iOS version
  • The app version
  • What you did and what happened
  • A screenshot if it is visual
