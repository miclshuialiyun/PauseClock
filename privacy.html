Overview Support Privacy Terms

The short version: PauseClock has no server and collects no personal data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. No account, no email, no sign-in.

No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.

2. What stays on your device

The following is stored locally in a single JSON file:

  • Fasting sessions
  • Weight entries
  • Your preferences
  • Purchase entitlement cache and event log

Delete it anytime in Settings → Data → Erase all data.

3. Apple Health

PauseClock reads and writes body weight only, and only on your explicit action.

  • Reading only avoids retyping.
  • Writing only when you turn it on.
  • Health data stays local, same as typed data.
  • Revoke anytime in iOS Settings.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications. No push server is involved.

5. Purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Apple.

A signed receipt is stored locally so Pro works offline.

6. Exporting and importing

Export hands a file to the iOS share sheet, under your control.

Import reads only the file you pick, after a preview.

7. Children

PauseClock is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

You exercise these rights directly, in the app:

  • Access and portability — export.
  • Correction — edit records.
  • Deletion — erase all data.

There is nothing held on our side.
